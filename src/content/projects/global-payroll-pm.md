## Overview

Led product for the core payroll infrastructure that processes millions in worker payments across 150+ countries monthly.

## Problem

Global payroll is a coordination problem at scale: each country has different tax rules, payment timelines, and compliance requirements. The existing system was built market-by-market and had accumulated years of inconsistency.

## Approach

- Conducted discovery interviews with operations, compliance, and finance teams across 8 markets
- Identified the common abstraction layer that could handle 80% of markets without customization
- Designed a rule-engine approach for country-specific logic rather than hardcoded branches
- Ran a 3-month pilot in 5 markets before rolling out globally

## Outcomes

- Reduced payroll processing errors by 40%
- Cut time-to-onboard a new country from 3 months to 3 weeks
- Enabled self-serve compliance updates without engineering involvement
